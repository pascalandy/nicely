# XT-101 video convert

## Demo

`ncly video convert <file>` converts one video through HandBrakeCLI after a dry run.

## Scope

The extension ships in [pascalandy/ncly-video-convert](https://github.com/pascalandy/ncly-video-convert), with its own versions.
[Epic 14, #333](https://github.com/pascalandy/nicely/issues/333) owns its work.
[COR-103](COR-103-verification-loop.md) holds its verification work, in cards T1, T14, and T15.
[UAT #346](https://github.com/pascalandy/nicely/issues/346) accepted v0.1.2 with reservations on 2026-10-28. [The ledger](../qa/ledger.md) lists its follow-ups.
